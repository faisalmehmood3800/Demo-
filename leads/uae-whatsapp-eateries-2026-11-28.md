# UAE eateries with a public WhatsApp number and no website (checked 28 Nov 2026)

Found from Google Maps searches in Karama, Deira, Al Qusais (Dubai), Sharjah and Ajman (about 140 places), keeping those with no website on Google, then searching the web for each. A lead is only here if the business itself published the number as WhatsApp (bio, page or Google listing). Full details: `uae-whatsapp-eateries-2026-11-28.csv`. Places with a mobile number but no public WhatsApp mention: `uae-backup-mobile-not-confirmed-2026-11-28.csv`.

Excluded because they have a website or are chains: Kulfilicious (kulfilicious.com), Smile Tea (10 branches), KP Chai (chain), Quetta Tea Cafe (a post lists a website), Al Pakistani Kitchen (the WhatsApp number found belongs to a different home-food seller).

Use only the number each business published for customers. Never use hiring numbers.
